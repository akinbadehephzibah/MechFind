How Mech Find works

Search → View profile → Contact → Completed job

Four steps. Under fifteen minutes from “my car just died” to a verified mechanic who has handled a job like yours this week.

The four steps

Designed for Nigerians. Built for panic moments.

No sign-up, no email gate, no app download. The whole journey works on a phone with a weak connection.

Search

Tell us your car make, your fault and your location. The directory filters down to verified mechanics near you.

View profile

Skills, car makes serviced, distance, verification tier, ratings, reviews and indicative pricing in Naira — before you call.

Contact

Tap to call or WhatsApp. Agree the scope and the price. Pay a deposit by transfer, POS or cash. They come to you, or you drive in.

Completed job

Workmanship warranty on Gold-tier bookings. Leave a review so the next person benefits from what you learned.

What you always see

Every profile answers the same six questions.

No listing goes live until all six are on file and confirmed. That consistency is what makes profiles comparable instead of decorative.

Open the directory

  • 1. What skills do you have?Engine, transmission, electrical, AC, brakes, suspension, bodywork, diagnostics.
  • 2. What car makes do you service?Toyota, Honda, Lexus, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, Kia, Nissan, Volkswagen, Peugeot, Mitsubishi, Ford, Land Rover, Suzuki.
  • 3. How far are you?Distance from the car owner’s location, shown on every result.
  • 4. Are you verified?A Bronze, Silver or Gold tier badge, earned and rechecked annually.
  • 5. What do you charge?Indicative Naira ranges, published before any contact is made.
  • 6. When are you available?A week view, so nobody loses a day to phone tag.

Payment

Three ways to pay. All of them yours to choose.

Mech Find does not sit between you and your money. You pay the mechanic directly, by whichever method you both agree.

Bank transfer

Paid into the mechanic’s verified business account, with a receipt issued. Easiest route if a claim ever needs escalating.

POS or card

Most Gold-tier mechanics carry a POS terminal. Pay on delivery of the car, without carrying large amounts of cash.

Cash on delivery

Inspect the work first, then pay. Standard for jobs under ₦50,000. Always ask for a written receipt, even for cash.

Mech Find takes nothing from the car owner

Our commission is charged to the mechanic on completed Gold-tier jobs, capped at ₦5,000. The price you agree is the price you pay.

What we ask of both sides

The rules that keep the platform honest.

What we ask of mechanics

  • Quote a scope and a price before starting work.
  • Issue a receipt on every job, cash included.
  • Use genuine parts, or say clearly when an alternative is fitted.
  • Honour the warranty attached to your tier.
  • Keep your availability on the profile current.

What we ask of car owners

  • Describe the fault as precisely as you can.
  • Agree the scope and price before work starts.
  • Keep the receipt and the booking reference.
  • Pay promptly once the work is done and inspected.
  • Leave an honest review, good or bad.
