Our story

Every project starts somewhere.

Ours started with ten people, a lot of different ideas, and one simple challenge: what real problem can we solve? We did not begin with a perfect concept. We began by talking. Sharing, questioning, and challenging each other’s thinking. Somewhere in those conversations we started looking at problems people meet in ordinary life.

One stood out: finding the right mechanic when your car develops a fault. At first it sounded simple — there are mechanics everywhere, so why should this be hard? But the more we asked who is affected, what does it cost, what already exists and why is that not enough, the clearer it became that the gap is real, deep, and worth solving properly.

Seven days

How MECH FIND was built.

  1. Days 1–3

    From problem to identity

    We discussed everyday problems and settled on mechanic discovery as the one worth investigating. Research followed: the experience of car owners and mechanics, the discovery channels that already exist, and the competing automotive platforms. On day three the idea got its name and its visual identity — a mark holding a car, a gear and a wrench together.

  2. Day 4

    Individual pages

    Planning moved into implementation. Each team member took an assigned page and built it in HTML and CSS, with the emphasis on structure, readability, consistency and the wider user experience.

  3. Day 5

    Completion and refinement

    Pages were reviewed and refined. We corrected issues, adjusted layouts and improved presentation before committing the finished work to our individual GitHub accounts.

  4. Day 6

    Integration

    Separate contributions were merged through GitHub into a single site, without losing the experience any individual page had been designed around.

  5. Day 7

    Launch and submission

    The site was prepared for submission and hosted. Project materials and the README were finalised, marking the move from an initial concept to a completed digital product.

Our values

What we refuse to compromise.

These five commitments decided every argument we had during the build, and they still decide them now.

  • Trust firstEvery badge, every review and every price is verified. Fakes are removed, not hidden.
  • Local tonePidgin welcome, Pidgin encouraged. Nigerians building for Nigerians.
  • No hidden feesYou pay the mechanic directly. We charge the mechanic, never the car owner.
  • AccountabilityIf something goes wrong we mediate. The trust signal is the platform, not just the profile.
  • SpeedA breakdown is urgent. Sixty-minute response, fourteen-minute average rescue.
