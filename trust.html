Trust is the whole point

Trust & Verification

We do not just list mechanics. We verify them.

A badge you can see, a tier you can check, and a verification process designed after interviewing 47 mechanics across six Nigerian cities.

Three tiers, one standard

Bronze, Silver and Gold.

Each tier is earned, never bought. Every mechanic is rechecked annually, and a badge can be revoked at any time.

Bronze

Baseline trust

Identity confirmed and one customer reference verified. The entry point to the network.

  • National ID or voter’s card on file
  • One verified customer reference
  • Phone number confirmed by call-back
  • Shop address recorded
  • Visible in directory search

Silver

Proven skill

Everything in Bronze, plus a practical skill assessment observed by a senior mechanic.

  • Two or more references confirmed
  • Skill test observed by a senior mechanic
  • Pricing discipline check
  • Practical diagnostic demonstration
  • Eligible for dispute escalation

Gold

Top of the network

Everything in Silver, plus a physical shop visit and a workmanship warranty we stand behind.

  • Recognised trade certification
  • In-person shop visit by Mech Find
  • 30-day workmanship warranty, backed by us
  • Genuine or OEM parts sourcing
  • Eligible as a roadside rescue partner

If something goes wrong

The complaint escalation path.

Mech Find does not go silent after the job. If a problem is not resolved with the mechanic directly, we step in at no cost to you.

Speak to the mechanic

Direct communication resolves most issues fastest. Quote the booking reference from your Mech Find receipt.

Send us the evidence

WhatsApp photographs, video and the receipt to our escalation line on +234 800 000 0001. We open a case the same day.

We mediate

We arrange a senior mechanic inspection, propose how the cost should be covered, and document the outcome on the mechanic’s record.

Badges can be revoked

Repeated upheld complaints result in a tier downgrade or removal from the directory. A bad mechanic on this platform is everyone’s problem, and we treat it that way.

Trust broken? Tell us quickly.

The sooner we hear about it, the more we can do. Escalation is free, and it is open at all hours.
