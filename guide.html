Guide & FAQ

Know more, lose less

Answers to the questions Nigerian car owners actually ask — in Naira, with real ranges, and no evasion. Figures are working estimates for 2026 and vary by city, make and parts availability.

Pricing

What repairs cost in Nigeria today.

Use these as a sanity check. If a quote sits far below the range and still promises a genuine part, ask for the part number.

How much does alternator replacement cost in Lagos?

A genuine Toyota or Honda alternator runs ₦45,000–₦85,000 for the part, plus ₦8,000–₦15,000 labour. Total: roughly ₦53,000–₦100,000.

If someone quotes below ₦35,000 and calls it an original part, ask to see the part number before agreeing.

How much is a full AC service?

Gas refill alone: ₦15,000–₦25,000. Compressor replacement including re-gassing: ₦120,000–₦220,000 depending on the make.

Avoid roadside “recharging” without a leak test — it usually fails again within weeks.

What should a transmission overhaul cost?

₦180,000–₦420,000 for a full overhaul including parts on Toyota and Lexus. Honda typically runs ₦150,000–₦300,000.

This is not a job for a general mechanic. Insist on a specialist with gearbox experience.

How much for paint and bodywork on a sedan?

Panel beating: around ₦25,000 per panel. Full respray: ₦180,000–₦320,000, with metallic finishes costing more.

Always ask for before-and-after photographs as part of the job.

What does roadside rescue cost?

Within Lagos: ₦5,000–₦15,000 for a battery jump-start or an on-the-spot fix. Towing runs ₦30,000–₦80,000 depending on distance. Abuja pricing is broadly similar.

Payment

How you pay, and who takes what.

Can I pay cash when the car is delivered?

Yes — after the work is done and you have inspected it. Always ask for a written receipt, even for cash. Every mechanic on the platform is required to issue one.

Can I pay by bank transfer or POS?

Most Gold and Silver mechanics accept both. Bank transfers should go to the mechanic’s business account, never a personal account, for any amount above ₦50,000.

Does Mech Find take my payment?

No. You pay the mechanic directly. Mech Find charges the mechanic a 5% commission on Gold-tier jobs sourced through us, capped at ₦5,000. Nothing is added to your bill.

What if a mechanic insists on cash only?

Bronze-tier mechanics often do, and we mark that clearly on the profile. Silver and Gold generally offer a choice. If cash-only makes you uncomfortable, filter for a higher tier in the directory.

Warranty

What happens after the job is done.

What warranty comes with a Mech Find job?

Gold tier: 30 days workmanship warranty, backed by us. Silver tier: 14 days, backed by the mechanic. Bronze tier: by arrangement, and usually none — agree it in writing before work starts.

My car broke down again five days after the repair. Now what?

Contact the mechanic first. If it is not resolved, WhatsApp our escalation line on +234 800 000 0001 with photographs and your receipt. For Gold-tier jobs we arrange a free senior inspection.

What counts as workmanship failure rather than wear and tear?

If a fitted part fails under normal use within the warranty window — an alternator dying inside 30 days, for example — that is workmanship, and it is covered. Damage from road conditions, impact or continued driving on a known fault is wear and tear, and that cost is yours.

Emergencies

What to do right now.

My car will not start and I am on the road.

One. Get the car to a safe spot, off the carriageway, hazards on.
Two. Call the rescue hotline on +234 800 000 0002, open 24 hours.
Three. Or open the directory and filter by roadside rescue to find the nearest partner yourself.

I have had an accident. What should I do before choosing a mechanic?

Photograph the scene before anything is moved. Get a police report if there are injuries or significant damage. Contact your insurer next. Do not hand the car to the first person who arrives quoting a price.

Fuel pump failure mid-journey — what are the first actions?

Pull over safely and switch off. Do not keep cranking the engine, which can make the damage worse. Call roadside rescue. Do not accept a towing price above ₦100,000 without comparing at least one other quote.

How do I know a part is genuine?

Ask for the part number and the box. Photograph both before fitting. Genuine parts carry consistent printing, a holographic or serialised label, and a price that matches the market range published above. A price far below the range is the clearest warning sign there is.
