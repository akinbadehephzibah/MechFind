The Problem

WHO IS AFFECTED. WHAT IT COSTS. WHY NOTHING HAS BEEN ENOUGH.

Every great project starts with a real question. Ours was simple: finding the wrong mechanic shouldn't be a Nigerian rite of passage. So we asked the four questions and refused to stop until we had a serious answer.

Our framework

Four questions honest answers built this.

01

Who is affected?

Every Nigerian driver of an imported or locally-assembled car. From the danfo driver whose Hiace breaks down mid-route, to the lawyer whose Lexus develops an electrical gremlin, to the trader whose Honda Accord alternator dies weeks after a "repair." That's roughly 22 million vehicles on Nigerian roads, with a vehicle-to-licensed-mechanic ratio estimated at 180:1.

02

What does it cost them?

It costs them twice. They pay once for a wrong repair (₦25,000–₦180,000 typical), then again for the correct one. They lose days of mobility — work, school runs, business deliveries. Trusted industry estimates suggest Nigerian households absorb roughly ₦450 billion/year in avoidable car repair costs due to misdiagnosis, fake parts and repeat failures.

03

What already exists?

Roadside mechanics. Town mechanics. Specialist workshops. Government-approved garages. Foreign car-brand authorised service centres. WhatsApp groups full of "my guy" referrals. National automotive council listings. They exist — but none connect car owners to the right specialist for a specific fault within a specific distance within a specific budget.

04

Why is it still not enough?

Because there is no trust signal beyond word of mouth. No system where you can see the mechanic's verified skills, their past work, their distances, their availability — and reach them in one click. Until now.

Pricing questions · asked daily

Pricing in Nigeria today.

How much does alternator replacement cost in Lagos?
A genuine Toyota / Honda alternator: ₦45,000–₦85,000 for the part + ₦8,000–₦15,000 labour. Total ₦53,000–₦100,000. If a quote is below ₦35,000 with "original part" — wahala, ask for the part number.
How much is full AC service in 2026?
Gas refill alone: ₦15,000–₦25,000. Compressor replacement + re-gassing: ₦120,000–₦220,000 depending on make. Avoid roadside "recharging" — it usually fails in weeks.
Transmission overhaul — what should I expect to pay?
₦180,000–₦420,000 for full overhaul + parts (Toyota/Lexus). Honda: ₦150,000–₦300,000. Always insist on a senior mechanic for this work.
How much for paint / bodywork on a sedan?
Panel beating: ₦25,000 per panel. Full repaint: ₦180,000–₦320,000. Metallic paint extra. Always ask for before/after photos.
Roadside rescue — how much?
Within Lagos: ₦5,000–₦15,000 for battery jump or on-the-spot fix. Towing: ₦30,000–₦80,000 depending on distance. Abuja pricing similar.
Payments

How you pay — three ways.

Can I pay cash on delivery of my car?
Yes. After the work is done and you've inspected it. Always ask for a written receipt — even for cash.
Can I pay by bank transfer or POS?
Most Gold and Silver mechanics accept both. Bank transfers must go to the mechanic's business account — never a personal account for amounts above ₦50,000.
Do you (Mech Find) take payment?
No — for now, you pay the mechanic directly. Mech Find only charges a 5% commission to the mechanic (capped at ₦5,000) for Gold-tier jobs sourced through us.
What if my mechanic insists on cash only?
Bronze-tier mechanics often do. We mark them clearly. Silver and Gold generally offer choice. If still uncomfortable, find another mechanic via the platform.
Warranty

What happens after the job is done.

What's the warranty on a Mechanic Find job?
Gold-tier mechanics: 30 days workmanship warranty backed by us. Silver-tier: 14 days, mechanic-backed. Bronze-tier: by arrangement, usually none.
My car broke down again 5 days after repair. Now what?
Contact the mechanic first. If unresolved, WhatsApp our escalation line (+234 800 000 0001). For Gold-tier jobs, we'll organise a free senior inspection.
What counts as workmanship vs. part failure?
If the part fails without use (e.g. alternator within 30 days), it's warranty. If you drove the car on bad roads and damaged it, that's wear and tear — your cost.
Emergencies

What to do right now.

My car won't start and I'm on the road. Help!
Step 1: Push to a safe spot. Step 2: Hit the rescue hotline +234 800 000 0000 (24/7). Step 3: Use "Roadside Rescue" filter on Find a Mechanic page.
I had an accident. What should I do before choosing a mechanic?
Take photos of the scene. Get a police report if there are injuries or significant damage. Then contact your insurance. Avoid the first "mechanic" who shows up quoting.
Fuel pump failure mid-journey — first actions?
Switch to reserve if possible. Pull over safely. Call roadside rescue. Don't accept towing pricing above ₦100,000 without comparison.
